# BCR-0013 — The poster page shows the image only

Status: accepted (2026-10-31)
Owner: lucas
Date: 2026-10-31

## Current behavior

The Viewer shows the poster sky, the caption band under it, and a "Figures in this sky" panel beside it. The caption repeats the heading above the image.

## Proposed behavior

The page shows the heading text and the sky image, then share, export and the actions. The caption band preview and the figures panel are removed from the page. Exploring figures is the Explorer's job.

## Why

Owner request: the page repeated itself, and the Explorer now covers figures in depth.

## What stays the same

Exported PNG, SVG and PDF files, including their caption band, byte for byte; `#s=` links; reference images.
